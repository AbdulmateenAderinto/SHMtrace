Class-switch junctions

Long reads across a switch join align in two parts: to Sμ, then to an acceptor. For each read: the breakpoints, the microhomology at the join (4 nt or more points to alternative end-joining), inserted bases, and mutations near the join. Reads of one join are counted once as a unique junction. Accuracy on simulated reads is in docs/calibration/SWITCH.md.

Repair routes from the spectrum

After AID, replication over U gives G:C transitions; UNG excision then translesion synthesis gives G:C transversions (REV1: C>G, G>C); MSH2–Pol η gives A:T mutations, more at WA than TW. These indices read the routes off each animal's unique mutations. Groups are compared animal by animal. The decomposition fits one group's spectrum as a mixture of reference groups' spectra (for example Ung⁻/⁻, Msh2⁻/⁻ or double knockouts sequenced alongside), with 95% intervals.
